# Customer FAQ

Frequently asked questions for customers making payments through SentinelGate.

---

## General

### What is SentinelGate?
SentinelGate is a secure payment gateway that processes payments for online stores. When you buy something from a merchant that uses SentinelGate, you may be directed to our checkout page to complete your payment.

### Is it safe to enter my card details?
Yes. SentinelGate uses industry-standard encryption (TLS 1.3) and never stores your card details. Card information is entered on a secure hosted form and processed directly by the payment provider. Your merchant never sees your card number.

### What payment methods can I use?
Depending on the merchant's configuration:
- **Credit/Debit Cards** — Visa, Mastercard
- **Mobile Money** — M-Pesa (Kenya), MTN/AirtelTigo/Telecel (Ghana)
- **Bank Transfer** — Available for select merchants

## Payment Issues

### My payment failed. What should I do?
Common reasons for failed payments:
- Insufficient funds on your card or mobile money account
- Incorrect card details (number, expiry, CVV)
- Card declined by your bank
- 3D Secure verification not completed
- Network timeout

**What to do:** Wait a few minutes and try again. If the issue persists, contact your bank or mobile money provider.

### I was charged but didn't get my order.
1. Check your email for a confirmation from the merchant
2. Wait 10–15 minutes — some orders take time to process
3. Contact the merchant directly with your order reference
4. If the merchant cannot help, contact us at support@sentinelgate.biz with your transaction reference

### I received a refund but it hasn't appeared.
Refund processing times:
- **Card payments:** 3–10 business days (depends on your bank)
- **Mobile money:** 1–3 business days
- **Bank transfer:** 3–5 business days

### Can I cancel a payment?
Once a payment is submitted, it cannot be cancelled. Contact the merchant to request a refund.

## Mobile Money

### I didn't receive the STK push / payment prompt.
- Check that you entered the correct phone number
- Ensure your phone has network signal
- Check your M-Pesa or mobile money app for pending requests
- Wait 30 seconds and try again

### The payment timed out.
Mobile money payments expire after 60 seconds if not confirmed. Start a new payment and respond to the prompt promptly.

## Security

### How do I know the payment page is real?
Look for:
- URL starts with `https://sentinelgate.biz/`
- Browser shows a padlock icon (SSL certificate)
- Amount and merchant name match what you expect

### Will the merchant see my card details?
No. Card details are entered on SentinelGate's secure form and never shared with the merchant. The merchant only receives confirmation that the payment was successful.
