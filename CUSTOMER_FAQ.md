# SentinelGate — Customer FAQ

**Frequently asked questions for customers making payments**

---

## Paying for Your Order

### How do I pay?

When you place an order, you'll be redirected to a secure payment page. Depending on the store, you may see options to pay by:

- **Credit or debit card** (Visa, Mastercard)
- **Mobile Money** (MTN, Vodafone Cash, AirtelTigo)
- **M-Pesa** (Kenya)

Enter your payment details and follow the on-screen instructions to complete your purchase.

### What payment methods are accepted?

This depends on the store you're buying from. Most stores accept Visa and Mastercard. Some stores also accept mobile money payments. The available options are shown on the payment page.

### Is it safe to enter my card details?

Yes. SentinelGate uses industry-standard security to protect your payment:

- **256-bit SSL encryption** — Your card details are encrypted during transmission
- **3D Secure verification** — Your bank may send a one-time code to your phone for additional security
- **No card storage** — Your full card number is never stored on our servers
- **PCI compliant** — We follow international payment card security standards

### I was asked for an OTP. What is that?

An OTP (One-Time Password) is a security code sent to your phone by your bank. This is part of **3D Secure** verification, which confirms that you (the cardholder) are the one making the purchase. Enter the code on the payment page to continue.

If you didn't receive the code:
- Wait 1-2 minutes — it may be delayed
- Check SMS messages (not WhatsApp or email)
- Click "Resend" on the payment page
- Contact your bank if it still doesn't arrive

## After Payment

### My payment went through. What happens next?

After a successful payment:

1. You'll see a **"Payment Successful"** confirmation on screen
2. You'll be redirected back to the store's order confirmation page
3. The store will receive your payment and process your order
4. You'll receive a confirmation email from the store (not from SentinelGate)

### I was charged but didn't get a confirmation page

This can happen if your browser closed or your internet connection dropped during the redirect. Don't worry — if the payment was successful, the store has been notified.

**What to do:**
1. Check your email for an order confirmation from the store
2. Check your bank statement or mobile money history to confirm the charge
3. Contact the store's customer support with your order number
4. If needed, provide the SentinelGate transaction ID (shown on the payment page, starts with `sg_txn_`)

### How do I get a receipt?

Your receipt comes from the store, not from SentinelGate. Check your email for a confirmation or order receipt from the store where you made your purchase.

## Payment Problems

### My card was declined. Why?

Common reasons for a declined payment:

| Reason | What To Do |
|--------|-----------|
| **Insufficient funds** | Check your balance and try again, or use a different card |
| **Card not enabled for online payments** | Contact your bank to enable online/e-commerce transactions |
| **Incorrect card details** | Double-check your card number, expiry date, and CVV |
| **Daily transaction limit reached** | Wait until the next day or contact your bank to increase your limit |
| **Bank security block** | Your bank may have blocked the transaction for security. Call your bank to approve it |
| **Expired card** | Use a card with a valid expiry date |

After fixing the issue, return to the store and try placing your order again.

### My mobile money payment failed

- **Insufficient balance:** Top up your mobile money wallet and try again
- **Wrong PIN:** Re-enter your mobile money PIN carefully
- **Network issue:** Wait a few minutes and try again
- **Transaction limit:** Check if you've reached your daily mobile money limit

### I was charged twice

This is very rare. SentinelGate has built-in protections against duplicate charges. If you believe you were charged twice:

1. Check your bank statement carefully — sometimes a "pending" charge and a "completed" charge appear separately but are the same transaction
2. Wait 24 hours — pending authorizations often clear automatically
3. If you still see a duplicate charge after 24 hours, contact the store with both transaction details

### The payment page shows a different currency than my order

Some payment providers convert currencies automatically. For example, if you ordered in USD but the payment page shows GHS (Ghana Cedis), the provider has converted the amount at their current exchange rate. You are being charged the correct equivalent amount in your local currency.

### The payment page is taking a long time to load

- Check your internet connection
- Try refreshing the page
- Clear your browser cache
- Try a different browser or device
- If the problem persists, return to the store and try placing the order again

## Refunds

### How do I get a refund?

Refunds are processed by the store, not by SentinelGate. Contact the store's customer support to request a refund according to their return policy.

### How long does a refund take?

Once the store processes your refund:

| Payment Method | Refund Timeline |
|---------------|-----------------|
| **Credit/debit card** | 5-10 business days |
| **Mobile Money** | 1-3 business days |
| **M-Pesa** | 1-2 business days |

The timeline depends on your bank or mobile money provider. SentinelGate processes refunds immediately, but it may take your financial institution several days to reflect the amount in your account.

### I received a refund but it's a different amount

If you paid in one currency and the refund is in another (or the amounts look different), this is due to currency conversion. Exchange rates fluctuate, so the refunded amount may be slightly different from what you originally paid.

## Privacy and Security

### What information does SentinelGate collect?

When you make a payment, SentinelGate processes:

- Your email address (to link the payment to your order)
- Your name (if provided by the store)
- Transaction amount and currency
- Payment method type (card, mobile money)

SentinelGate does **not** store your full card number or CVV. These are sent directly to the payment provider and discarded.

### Who can see my payment information?

- **The store** sees your order details and payment status (success/failed)
- **The payment provider** (e.g., Hubtel, Paystack) processes your card or mobile money payment
- **SentinelGate** sees the transaction amount, status, and your email — not your full card details

### I don't recognize a charge on my statement

If you see a charge from "SentinelGate", "Hubtel", or a similar name that you don't recognize:

1. Think about recent online purchases — the merchant name on your statement may differ from the store name
2. Check your email for order confirmations
3. Contact your bank for more details about the charge
4. If it's truly unauthorized, contact your bank to dispute the charge

## Contact

### I need help with my payment

**First, contact the store** where you made your purchase. They can look up your order and check the payment status.

**If the store can't help**, they can contact SentinelGate support on your behalf at support@sentinelgate.biz.

**Please have ready:**
- The store name and your order number
- The approximate date and amount of the payment
- The transaction ID if you have it (starts with `sg_txn_`)
